# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We will maintain a professional and safe project community.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical work with respect.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- State uncertainty.
- Give credit.
- Protect private and security-sensitive information.
- Accept correction.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment.
- Threats.
- Discrimination.
- Deliberate humiliation.
- Publication of private information.
- Unsafe publication of a vulnerability.
- Repeated disruption of project work.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems to the maintainers through a private published channel.

The maintainers can warn, restrict, suspend, or remove a participant. They must consider context, effect, and repeated behavior.

This initial policy will be replaced or expanded with a recognized community standard before the first public release.
