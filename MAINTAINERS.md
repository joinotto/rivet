## Maintainer authority

Maintainers can triage issues, review and merge changes, prepare releases, and
respond to private vulnerability reports. `GOVERNANCE.md` defines decision
and nomination rules.

A maintainer must record a conflict of interest and must not approve a change
when the conflict prevents independent judgment.

Security-sensitive release code requires two qualified reviewers when two are
available. A temporary lack of a second qualified reviewer does not convert
one review into two-person approval. Record the limitation and defer the
release when the risk requires independent approval.

`docs/BRANCH-PROTECTION.md` records the enforced repository settings and the
current single-maintainer approval limitation.

## Changes

Record maintainer additions and removals in the repository history. Include
the decision evidence, effective date, responsibilities, and succession or
access-removal actions.
